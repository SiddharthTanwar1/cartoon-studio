# Your Brain Is Confidently Making This Weird

[Full import-ready script](SCRIPT.md) · [Research and sources](RESEARCH.md) · [New cast preview](NEW-CAST.png)

One combined explainer covering all five requested phenomena. It has **1,079 spoken words, 13 scenes and six reusable backgrounds**. At 155 words/minute with the marked pauses, the scenes take about **7:27**; opening and end cards bring the estimate to **7:37–7:42**. Actual voice timing will vary. The five pairs of topic scenes can also be used as short standalone segments.

## Use it in Cartoon Studio

1. Refresh the editor after the new cast is activated. Under **Scene queue**, choose **Upload full script** and select this folder’s **SCRIPT.md**.
2. Review the 13 scenes and click **Add imported scenes to queue**. Cast, props, set prompts, dialogue and per-line directions are filled automatically.
3. Select the scenes you want and click **Render selected**. Matching set prompts reuse the same background. Each scene exports separately; there is no joining step.
4. Audition the first lines spoken by each new character. Voice descriptions are installed; their audio is generated on the first render. Keep each character’s name, description and seed unchanged to reuse that voice.

Mira is the recurring explanatory host; Milo and Nova open and close the episode. The five new characters appear across the five topics. We have not created a queue, rendered the film, or replaced your saved scene.

## Direct the episode

Keep Mira measured and the others conversational. Allow the pauses after punchlines instead of adding exaggerated laughter. The script uses each analogy consistently: spotlight, pizza review, group chat, Control F/searchlight, and browser tabs.

The rig performs talking, expressions, gestures and camera cuts. Its characters do not actually bake, drive, move through a crowd, or type. Those details are conveyed through dialogue and backgrounds. Opening titles, overlays, source cards and any music belong in your finishing edit. Put the research links in the video description.
