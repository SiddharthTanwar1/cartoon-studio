# Research: From Steam to Software

Checked 6 October 2026. Museum collections, institutional histories, manufacturer technical documents and public research/education sources support the factual claims. Manufacturer histories are used for documented milestones, not as proof of promotional first-ever or best-ever claims. All dialogue, comic comparisons and fictional conversations are original.

This is a detailed introductory documentary, not a complete repair manual or an exhaustive list of every car, patent or engine variant. The engineering explanations intentionally identify typical designs and their exceptions.

## H1 — Cugnot and steam road vehicles

Scenes: 2.

[Musée des Arts et Métiers: Fardier à vapeur](https://www.arts-et-metiers.net/musee/fardier-vapeur), [Museum transport collection](https://www.arts-et-metiers.net/collections/transports-objets-phares).

Distinguish the 1769 prototype from the surviving 1770–1771 vehicle. Avoid presenting every disputed “first car” as settled, or repeating the alleged first crash.

## H2 — Otto’s practical four-stroke engine

Scenes: 3.

[DEUTZ engine history](https://www.deutz.com/en/company/deutz-tradition-engagement/technikum/).

1876 is the practical compressed-charge four-stroke milestone used here. This is not a claim that Otto invented every precursor or the whole automobile.

## H3 — Benz and parallel development

Scenes: 3.

[Mercedes-Benz: 1885–1886](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/company/tradition/company-history/1885-1886.html), [Daimler historic vehicles](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/vehicles/mercedes-benz-classic/classic-cars/vehicles-daimler-1886-1926/).

The Benz vehicle was completed in 1885; the patent application followed in 1886. The script explicitly acknowledges predecessors and parallel inventors rather than adopting the manufacturer’s broad “first automobile” headline.

## H4 — Bertha Benz’s journey

Scenes: 4.

[Mercedes-Benz: Bertha Benz](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/company/tradition/founders-pioneers/bertha-benz.html).

## H5 — Diesel development

## H7 — Model T introduction

Scenes: 14.

## H8 — Moving assembly line and chart

Scenes: 14.

[Library of Congress: Ford implements the moving assembly line](https://guides.loc.gov/this-month-in-business-history/october/ford), [Ford: Assembly Line Revolution](https://corporate.ford.com/articles/history/moving-assembly-line.html).

Reported comparison: 12.5 hours versus 93 minutes. Chart conversion: 12.5 × 60 = 750 minutes. This is assembly time, not total cradle-to-finished-vehicle production time; the historical comparison does not isolate every contributing factory change.

## H9 — Hydra-Matic milestone

Scenes: 15.

[GM innovation milestones](https://media.gm.com/dld/content/product/public/us/en/500m/home/_jcr_content/par/sectioncontainer_2/par/download_0/file.res/GM-FACTS-INNOVATION-MILESTONES.pdf), [GM archival Cadillac specifications, 1942](https://www.gm.com/content/dam/company/no_search/heritage-archive-docs/vehicle-information-kits/cadillac/1942_Cadillac_VVI.pdf).

1940 is the Oldsmobile model year; do not confuse this with an introduction announcement in 1939. The early Hydra-Matic used a fluid coupling, unlike later torque-converter designs.

## H10 — Radial tyre milestone

Scenes: 17.

[Michelin heritage](https://www.michelin.com/en/group/heritage).

1946 patent date. Casing construction is distinct from tread pattern; radial construction does not establish wet-weather performance by itself.

## H11 — Modern three-point seat belt

Scenes: 19.

[Volvo: three-point safety belt](https://www.volvogroup.com/en/about-us/heritage/three-point-safety-belt.html).

1959, Nils Bohlin’s modern three-point design. Seat belts existed earlier. Avoid unsupported lives-saved estimates.

## H12 — Electronic fuel injection

Scenes: 20.

[Bosch: history of electronics](https://www.bosch.com/stories/history-of-electronics/).

Jetronic series production in 1967. Do not claim all fuel injection began in 1967; mechanical systems preceded electronic systems.

## H13 — Oil shocks and emissions controls

Scenes: 21.

The 1975 converter reference is explicitly US-specific. Early converters and later three-way systems are not identical. Do not generalise US introduction dates to every country.

## H14 — Indian motoring milestone

## M7 — Clutch and transmission

Scenes: 10, 15.

[ZF passenger-car clutches](https://aftermarket.zf.com/us/aftermarket-portal/our-portfolio/passenger-cars/products/clutches/).

Clutch operation is described conceptually. The low-gear explanation assumes a conventional reduction ratio and does not claim gears increase power.

## M8 — Differentials

Scenes: 11.

[Eaton differential principles](https://www.eaton.com/us/en-us/products/differentials-traction-control.html).

The outside wheel travels farther on a turn. The open-differential limitation is expressed as available torque, avoiding the misleading blanket claim that all power always goes to the spinning wheel.

## M9 — Steering assistance

Scenes: 11.

[Bosch electric power steering](https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/solutions/steering/electric-power-steering-systems/).

Rack-and-pinion is a common example, not the only mechanism. Power assistance and autonomous steering are different concepts.

## M10 — Body structure and packaging

Scenes: 15.

[Citroën heritage: Traction 7](https://www.citroenorigins.co.uk/en/cars/traction-7), [Citroën historical engineering overview](https://www.citroen.co.uk/content/dam/citroen/uk/b2c/about-citroen/making-history/pdf/retromobile_uk.pdf).

Provides historical context for integrated construction and front-wheel drive. No broad “first-ever unitary car” claim is adopted. Frame and unitised construction remain parallel design choices.

## M11 — Springs and damping

Scenes: 17.

[Monroe: how shocks and struts work and wear](https://aftermarket.monroe.com/technical-resources/shocks-101/what-causes-shocks-struts-to-wear-out.html).

Springs permit relative wheel/body movement; dampers dissipate oscillation energy. The script does not imply replacing a damper automatically guarantees grip.

## M12 — Friction and hydraulic brakes

Scenes: 18.

[Brembo hydraulic components](https://www.bremboparts.com/america/en/products/essential/hydraulic-components), [Brembo braking-system principles](https://www.brembo.com/en/news-archive/5-factors-that-make-a-brembo-braking-system-unbeatable).

General disc/drum and hydraulic principles only. Friction braking dissipates kinetic energy; the tyre-road contact transmits the forces that slow the vehicle.

## M13 — Crash protection

Scenes: 19.

[NHTSA air bags](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/air-bags), [Mercedes-Benz: safety-cell and crumple-zone history](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/company/tradition/company-history/history-esf.html).

Airbags supplement belts. Controlled deformation and a protected occupant space are different structural roles; rigid everywhere does not mean safe everywhere.

## M14 — Catalytic conversion

Scenes: 21.

[Bosch lambda sensor and three-way catalyst history](https://www.bosch.com/stories/the-history-of-the-bosch-lambda-sensor/).

The script avoids calling CO₂ harmless in every sense. Catalyst effectiveness depends on temperature, mixture and condition; converter presence does not imply elimination of all pollutants.

## M15 — Turbocharging

Scenes: 22.

[Garrett: how a turbo works](https://www.garrettmotion.com/de/turbocharger-technology/how-a-turbo-works/basic/), [Garrett intercooler principles](https://www.garrettmotion.com/news/newsroom/article/garrett-performance-intercoolers-heat-transfer-and-selecting-the-correct-core/).

Exhaust drives a turbine coupled mechanically to the intake compressor; the streams are separate. Recovering exhaust energy is not lossless or free power.

## M16 — Hybrid operation and power split

Scenes: 16, 25, 26.

[DOE AFDC: hybrid cars](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/how-do-hybrid-electric-cars-work), [Toyota Technical Review: hybrid transmission power split](https://global.toyota/pages/global_toyota/mobility/technology/toyota-technical-review/TTR_Vol54-1_E.pdf).

Architectures differ. Conventional hybrids and plug-in hybrids are separate categories. Toyota-style power-split e-CVT operation is not a belt CVT.

## M17 — Battery-electric vehicle components

Scenes: 26, 28.

[DOE AFDC: all-electric cars](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/how-do-all-electric-cars-work).

Battery, power electronics, motor, reduction drive, thermal management and low-voltage functions. No claim that every EV has identical gearing or identical regeneration capabilities.

## M18 — Electric motor principles

Scenes: 28.

[DOE electric motors research](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/electric-motors-research-and-development).

Stator and rotor are distinguished. The explanation accommodates permanent-magnet and induction approaches rather than assuming all motors work identically.

## M19 — Battery chemistry

Scenes: 29.

[DOE explains batteries](https://www.energy.gov/science/doe-explainsbatteries), [DOE: Esther Takeuchi on battery operation](https://www.energy.gov/science/articles/first-person-science-esther-takeuchi-batteries).

Ions travel internally, electrons through the external circuit. The battery stores chemical potential energy; the script’s bottled-lightning phrase is explicitly rejected.

## M20 — Charging and units

Scenes: 29, 30.

[DOE AFDC: charging stations](https://afdc.energy.gov/fuels/electricity-stations).

kW is power; kWh is energy. Vehicle, battery and charging-equipment limits affect the actual charging rate.

## M21 — EV use and range

Scenes: 30.

[DOE AFDC: EVs for consumers](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/electric-consumers).

No promised universal charge time or driving range. Conditions and accessory demand affect use; specific vehicles require their own ratings.

## M22 — Life-cycle emissions

Scenes: 31.

[DOE AFDC: EV emissions](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/electric-emissions).

Zero combustion tailpipe emissions is not zero life-cycle impact. The script gives no universal percentage advantage because electricity mix, manufacturing and vehicle assumptions matter.

## M23 — Driver assistance versus automation

Level 2 requires active supervision. No claim that every system marketed as self-driving is driverless, or that no limited-domain driverless services exist. Information checked 6 October 2026.

## M24 — Starter and alternator

Scenes: 8.

[DENSO rotating electrical systems](https://www.denso-am.eu/products/rotating-electrics), [DENSO starter/alternator technical manual](https://assets.denso-am.eu/production/attachments/Starter-Alternator-Manual-English.pdf).

## M26 — Belt-and-pulley CVT

Scenes: 16.

[Nissan technical CVT overview](https://www.nissan-global.com/PDF/tcvt_e.pdf), [Nissan XTRONIC CVT](https://www.nissan-global.com/EN/INNOVATION/TECHNOLOGY/ARCHIVE/NEW_CVT/).

Variable effective pulley diameter explains the mechanical ratio change. Distinguish this from the hybrid power-split case in M16.

## P1 — Torque and rotational mechanics

Scenes: 10.

[OpenStax rotational motion](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/6-3-rotational-motion).

Torque is a turning effect; rotational power depends on torque and angular speed. The bicycle gearing comparison is an original teaching analogy.

## P2 — Kinetic-energy chart

Scenes: 18.

[OpenStax kinetic energy](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-2-kinetic-energy).

For a fixed mass, KE = ½mv². Using 30 km/h as baseline yields (30/30)² = 1, (60/30)² = 4, (90/30)² = 9. The graph is a mathematical comparison, not measurements, braking distances or injury predictions.

## P3 — Aerodynamic drag

Scenes: 31.

[NASA Glenn: drag equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/drag-equation/).

The square-of-speed statement holds with density, reference area and drag coefficient treated as otherwise similar. Drag force and power required to overcome drag are different quantities.

## Production and fact-check rules

Keep the on-screen source captions. Put full links in the video description or an accompanying bibliography. Do not crop a joke into a false historical quotation. Retain qualifications about chronology, regional introductions, simplified mechanisms and the difference between computed values and measurements. All dates refer to the milestones described, not universal adoption.
