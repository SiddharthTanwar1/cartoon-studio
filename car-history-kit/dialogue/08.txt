Ellie: If an engine must turn to run, what turns it before it is running?

Mira: In a conventional petrol car, the battery powers a starter motor. It turns the engine until combustion can sustain operation; then the starter disengages.

Ellie: The engine needs someone to get it out of bed. Relatable.

Mira: Once running, the engine commonly drives an alternator. It supplies electrical power and replenishes the small vehicle battery through a regulated charging system.

Mira: That is not the same as an electric car’s large traction battery. Some hybrids also use different starter-generator arrangements.

Ellie: Starter gets the party going. Alternator keeps the lights on. Neither is powered by the confidence of the person turning the key.
