## Regression and setup checks

Seven tests passed, including all camera/aspect combinations, every expression/gesture combination, script validation, numeric dialogue matching, silent mouth closure, timeline continuity and cached voice manifest consistency. Launcher scripts passed shell syntax validation. The launcher includes the Homebrew path needed when opened from Finder.

The renderer lives in its own installed environment. It uses the existing image, audio, model and Whisper installations through explicit local paths. Installed versions are recorded in `installation-report.json`; renderer dependencies are pinned in `requirements.txt`.

No paid APIs, rented GPUs, or new model-weight downloads were used. Small free rendering/audio utility packages were installed. No source-video frames, voices, music or logos are included in the finished examples.

## What these checks do not establish

They establish that this local workflow produces working, editable cartoon dialogue videos on the tested machine. They do not establish parity with the source animator’s bespoke artwork/action animation, phoneme-perfect lip sync, arbitrary character rig generation, or support for every possible scene. Those scope boundaries are described in `README.md` and `REFERENCE-STUDY.md`.
