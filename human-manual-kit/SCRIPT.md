One family-friendly comedy explainer. Fictional characters, original jokes and analogies; evidence and qualifications in RESEARCH.md. The first two topics address girls and women about boys and men; the next two address boys and men about girls and women. Lessons can benefit everyone.

Built for Cartoon Studio full-script import with existing avatars, two speakers per scene, three reusable sets and no new props. Six labelled explainer scenes use a central diagram; the other six use the original dialogue layout. Directions are not spoken. Source IDs appear only in Notes.
